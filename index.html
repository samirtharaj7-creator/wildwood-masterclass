Diabetes doesn't have to keep getting worse

Most people with type 2 diabetes are told to expect the same path: one pill, then two, then insulin. Their A1C creeps up a little every year.

That isn't what we usually see at Wildwood. When guests change how they eat, walk every day, drink more water and get proper sleep, their blood sugar often starts coming down within days. Many go home on less medication, and some go home on none, with their doctor's approval.

This class explains how that works and what you can do about it at home.

What we'll cover

  1. Why your blood sugar keeps climbing, even when you take your medication and try to eat less sugar.
  2. What to eat, and what to cut out, to bring your A1C down. We'll keep it simple and affordable.
  3. How walking, water and sleep help your body respond to insulin again.
  4. How to work with your doctor as your numbers improve, so your medication can be adjusted safely.

Is this class right for you?

This class is for you if

  • You have type 2 diabetes or prediabetes
  • Your A1C keeps rising, or your doctor wants to add another medication
  • You're on insulin and want to know if you could use less of it
  • Someone in your family has diabetes and you want to help them

This class isn't for you if

  • You're looking for a pill or supplement that fixes diabetes on its own
  • You're not willing to change what you eat
  • Your blood sugar is very high or very low right now. Please call your doctor first.

About Wildwood

Wildwood Lifestyle Center has been helping people recover from chronic disease for over 80 years. Our clinic team treats patients with plant-based nutrition, exercise, natural remedies and, when it's needed, conventional medicine.

We're a Christian ministry, and we care for the whole person. This class is a free introduction to the approach we use at the center.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no charge and you don't need a credit card to sign up.

What if I can't make the live class?

Sign up anyway. We'll email everyone the recording afterwards.

Should I stop taking my medication?

No. Please don't change any medication without talking to your doctor. As your blood sugar improves, your doctor can adjust your dose. We'll talk about how to bring this up with them.

I have type 1 diabetes. Is this for me?

Type 1 can't be reversed, but the same habits can still help with blood sugar control and overall health. You're welcome to join.

Do I need to be a Seventh-day Adventist?

No. Everyone is welcome.

What happens after the class?

If you want more help, we'll tell you about our programs at the center and online. There's no obligation.
