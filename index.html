Questions

Frequently asked

Is the masterclass really free?

Yes. There's no cost to attend, and you don't need a credit card to sign up.

What if I can't attend live?

Register anyway. Everyone who signs up gets a replay link by email.

Do I need to stop my medication?

No. Never change or stop medication without talking to your doctor. The masterclass covers lifestyle changes that support your health alongside your medical care.

Is this only for Seventh-day Adventists?

Not at all. Wildwood welcomes everyone. The principles are rooted in faith and shared with respect for all backgrounds.

What happens after the masterclass?

You'll leave with a simple plan to start at home. If you'd like more support, you'll also hear about Wildwood's virtual and on-site programs. No pressure.
